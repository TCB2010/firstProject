<h3>White Bread</h3>

	<img src="https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcTcJP8uHs4NKCgXbTlSa_7z-tf5t3doeY99ZyiqOJXzGQ&s=10"/>

<h4 class="ingredients">Ingredients</h4>
<ul>
	<li>11c. flour</li>
	<li>2 T. yeast</li>
	<li>6 T. sugar</li>
	<li>6 T. oil</li>
	<li>4T salt</li>
	<li>1 c. milk</li>
	<li>3 c. water</li>
</ul> 

<h4 class="steps">Steps</h4>
	<ol>
		<li>Put water in a kettle and turn on to heat up.</li>
		<li>Put 4 cups of flour and all other dry ingredients into a very large bowl and stir.</li>
		<li>Pour oil and milk into large glass measuring cup.</li>
		<li>Start adding the 3 cups of hot water.</li>
		<li>Mix the liquids into the flour with a large spoon.</li>
		<li>Pour remaining flour into the bowl, stirring as you go along.</li>
		<li>Stop adding flour or add more flour to get a consistency where it's no longer sticking to your fingers. </li>
		<li>Knead about ten minutes. You can't overkned when you are doing it by hand</li>
		<li>Stop when it's satiny and springs back right away when you press on it</li>
		<li>Pour oil around the edge of the bowl. Spin the dough around in the oil to cover the dough and the bowl with oil</li>
		<li>Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and then a towel</li>
		<li>After it has doubled in size, punch it down and form it how you'd like to cook it: loaf, bread sticks, rolls, etc.</li>
		<li>Let it rise another 40 minutes before baking at 400 degrees in the oven for 20 minutes</li>
